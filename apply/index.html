Harvard

All four are open to you as an enrolled student. The two that involve being paid — the tutor role, and any i-lab or Bok-style staffing that follows — are on-campus employment, which your F-1 already authorises without separate work permission; the other two are a membership and a fellowship, so the question does not arise. Confirm hours with the HIO before you sign anything.

Harvard i-lab — Student Membership → 16 Sep 2026

Harvard Innovation LabsIncubationAllston

Free membership for students across all thirteen Harvard schools. Members get a staff advisor and a placement in one of four cohorts — the Health & Life Sciences cohort is yours. The portal opened 17 August 2026 and stays open until 17 July 2027, but 16 September is the fall priority date that puts you in the autumn cohort and New Member Orientation.

Why youRadGame is already a working platform with a published user study behind it, which is further than most ventures that walk in. Membership is also the front door to the President’s Innovation Challenge below — join now and you are inside the pipeline when that call opens.

Kempner Institute Graduate Fellowship → Opens Dec 2026 your cycle

Kempner Institute, HarvardFellowshipCambridge

Four years of stipend, tuition, health insurance and fees, plus access to one of the largest academic ML clusters anywhere and the Kempner mentoring and seminar community. Open to incoming or current second-year Harvard PhD students. The next round of applications opens December 2026; the previous cycle’s deadline was 15 February.

Why youThe highest-value item on the page, and the timing is exactly right — you started in 2026, so you are an incoming second-year for the round that opens this December. No citizenship condition anywhere in the eligibility, which matters given how much else on this page turns on a passport. Harmony is a natural-and-artificial-intelligence story if you frame the representation-learning side rather than the medical application, which is what the Kempner mission statement rewards.

Start early. It needs a 750-word research statement and a letter of support from Rajpurkar. Ask him in October, not in February.

President’s Innovation Challenge 2027 → Expected Dec 2026 2027 call unpublished

Harvard Innovation LabsVenture competitionAllston

Student founders from all thirteen Harvard schools compete for a share of more than $500,000 funded by the Bertarelli Foundation, across three tracks — Social Impact, Health Care & Life Sciences, and Open. Entrants get advising, judge feedback and pitch coaching along the way; semifinalists are announced in mid-January. The 2026 round closed 8 December 2025 at 11:59pm ET, so expect the 2027 call at roughly the same point this December.

Why youRadGame is the rare student venture with a peer-reviewed evaluation behind it — ML4H 2025, ECR 2026 oral, and a user study showing 68% versus 17% on localisation. The Health Care & Life Sciences track is exactly where that evidence counts, and no other item on this page pays out at this scale for work you have already done.

Date not confirmed. Harvard has not published the 2027 timeline yet — this is inferred from the 2026 cycle. The i-lab site blocks automated readers, so check it directly in early November, or ask your i-lab advisor once membership is active.

Non-Resident Tutor, 2027–28 → 5 Jan 2027

Harvard College housesAdvising roleCambridge

The central Resident / Non-Resident Tutor application for 2027–28 opens in fall 2026 — historically going live in early December — with a priority deadline of 5 January 2027. Most tutors are hired between December and early April, and NRT hiring runs past the resident process. Individual houses also run their own searches: Dudley posts open NRT positions year-round and Mather has taken rolling applications through August.

Why youYou have done this at Leverett, so this is a renewal or a lateral move rather than a cold application, and returning tutors are the easiest hires a house makes. Worth emailing the Leverett Faculty Deans in November rather than waiting for the central portal.

One change to check. Harvard began enforcing tutor eligibility requirements in February 2026 and tightened rules on unaffiliated house tutors. Confirm the current criteria still cover you as a PhD student before assuming last year’s route works.

MATS Winter 2027 → 6 Sep 2026

ML Alignment & Theory ScholarsResearch fellowshipBerkeley / London

Full-time research programme, 19 Jan – 10 Apr 2027, pairing you with a senior mentor to produce a paper. $19.2k stipend, $24k compute, free housing and food, travel covered. Stage 1 applications close 6 September; the Neel Nanda stream closes two days earlier, on 4 September.

Why youEmpirical interpretability and evaluation work is close to what CRIMSON already does — building a clinically grounded metric is an evaluation problem before it is a radiology problem. It is also the cheapest way to buy a real second research network outside Rajpurkar’s lab.

Soonest deadline on the page. It runs through the spring semester full-time, so it needs an arrangement with DBMI. MATS covers the J-1 outright, so unlike most of this category neither of your passports is the obstacle — the calendar is.

Anthropic AI for Science — Claude credits → Rolling next review 7 Sep

AnthropicCompute / API grantRemote

Free Claude API credits for researchers at academic and non-profit institutions. Anthropic’s help centre states up to $20,000 in credits over six months; press coverage of the 27 August 2026 expansion reports up to $50,000 per project and a widening of scope beyond biology into medicine, healthcare and computer science. Submissions are evaluated on the first Monday of each month.

Why youCRIMSON is an LLM-as-judge metric and RadGame generates automated feedback — both are pure API burn. This is the single highest value-per-hour application on this page: one form, no letters, no nomination, no citizenship condition, and nothing to relocate for.

Do this one first. Next evaluation date is Monday 7 September 2026. Applying is maybe an hour of work.

Anthropic Fellows Program → Rolling Jan 2027 cohort

AnthropicResearch fellowshipOntario / SF / London / US-remote

Four months full-time on an empirical AI-safety project with an Anthropic mentor, aimed at a public paper. $3,850 per week plus roughly $15,000 a month in compute. Applications are rolling; the next cohort starts January 2027 and a further cohort begins August 2027, with that call reported to reopen in September 2026. No PhD or prior ML publishing is required.

Why youOver 40% of fellows convert to full-time roles. Anthropic states it cannot sponsor visas and requires existing work authorisation in the US, UK or Canada — and it runs an Ontario site. Your Canadian citizenship satisfies that outright, which moves this from a non-starter to one of the strongest things on the page.

The catch is now logistical, not legal. Taking the Ontario route means four months full-time outside the US mid-PhD, so it needs a leave arrangement with DBMI and a plan for re-entry on your F-1. Worth one conversation each with the programme office and the HIO before you write anything.

Cohere Labs Scholars Program → Opens fall 2026 missed by two days

Cohere LabsResearch fellowshipRemote-first · Toronto

A paid, full-time programme starting each January that pairs emerging researchers with a Cohere Labs mentor to publish real ML research. The last round closed 29 August 2026. Cohere’s own page now says applications are expected to open again in fall 2026.

You also already have Toronto research history through Bo Wang and the Vector Institute, which is not nothing when they read your name. Set a reminder for late September and check the page weekly.
