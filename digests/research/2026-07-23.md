## Top Medical AI Papers (Full Summary)

### 1. [Self-supervision drives representational convergence in medical foundation models more than clinical supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20274)
**Authors:** Soroosh Tayebi Arasteh, Sebastian Ziegelmayer, Mahshad Lotfinia, Lisa Adams et al. (7 authors)  
**arXiv:** `2607.20274`  
**Tags:** Medical Foundation Models, Self-Supervised Learning, Representation Analysis

**TL;DR:** A controlled dissection shows that self-supervised pretraining, more than clinical label supervision, is what drives medical image encoders toward a shared representational structure.

**Problem:** Medical image encoders from different groups are increasingly treated as interchangeable on the assumption that scale and clinical supervision converge their representations, but whether this convergence is real, what causes it, and whether it is clinically usable have not been rigorously tested.

**Method:** The authors run a controlled comparison of medical image encoders using robust representational similarity measures, isolating the contribution of self-supervised objectives versus clinical supervision to representational convergence and evaluating downstream clinical usability.

**Results:** They find that self-supervision, rather than clinical supervision, is the dominant driver of representational convergence across medical foundation models, and they characterize when this shared structure is clinically meaningful.

**Why it matters:** This directly addresses the reader's core interests in self-supervised learning and medical foundation models, offering evidence on how to build and compare medical image encoders.

### 3. [SHFormer: Dynamic Spectral Filtering Convolutional Neural Network and High-pass Kernel Generation Transformer for Adaptive MRI Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20159)
**Authors:** Sriprabha Ramanarayanan, Rahul G. S., Mohammad Al Fahim, Keerthi Ram et al. (6 authors)  
**arXiv:** `2607.20159`  
**Tags:** MRI Reconstruction, Transformer Architecture, Medical Imaging

**TL;DR:** SHFormer combines a dynamic spectral-filtering CNN with a high-pass kernel-generation transformer for adaptive accelerated MRI reconstruction.

**Problem:** Attention-based models help accelerated MRI reconstruction by capturing non-local Fourier-domain relationships, but they are better at low-frequency content and struggle to recover high-frequency detail.

**Method:** SHFormer pairs a dynamic spectral-filtering convolutional network with a transformer that generates high-pass kernels, jointly modeling non-local low-frequency structure and locally adaptive high-frequency detail for reconstruction.

**Results:** The hybrid design yields improved adaptive MRI reconstruction quality relative to attention-only baselines, particularly on fine high-frequency structure.

**Why it matters:** MRI reconstruction methods and vision architectures for medical imaging are close to the reader's imaging and computer-vision interests.


## Top General AI Papers (Full Summary)

### 1. [Test-Time Training for Modality Order Consistency in Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20351)
**Authors:** Aditi Gupta, Yossi Gandelsman  
**arXiv:** `2607.20351`  
**Tags:** Vision-Language Models, Test-Time Training, Robustness

**TL;DR:** Vision-language models are systematically biased by whether the image or the question comes first, and an order-consistent test-time training method fixes it.

**Problem:** VLMs are sensitive to a semantically irrelevant change — the order in which image and question are presented — with image-first prompting consistently beating question-first, revealing a repeatable modality-order failure.

**Method:** The authors exploit the image-first/question-first performance gap as a self-supervision signal and design an order-consistent test-time training procedure that enforces consistent predictions across modality orderings.

**Results:** Across three models and three benchmarks the method reduces the modality-order sensitivity and improves robustness.

**Why it matters:** Vision-language models are central to the reader's interest in CLIP-style multimodal foundation models, and this reveals a general failure mode with a lightweight fix.


### 2. [ELSAA: Efficient Low-Rank and Sparse Attention Approximation for Training Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20214)
**Authors:** Mahdi Heidari, Mohammad Mahdi Rahimi, Jaekyun Moon  
**arXiv:** `2607.20214`  
**Tags:** Efficient Attention, Transformer Architecture, Low-Rank Approximation

**TL;DR:** ELSAA combines low-rank and sparse attention approximation to train transformers efficiently on longer sequences.

**Problem:** The quadratic N×N attention score matrix blocks scaling transformers to long inputs; existing efficient methods use either sparsity or low-rank/kernel sketches but rarely both effectively.

**Method:** ELSAA jointly approximates attention with a combined low-rank plus sparse decomposition, capturing compressed global interactions while preserving important local sparse structure, for efficient transformer training.

**Results:** The approach reduces the attention bottleneck while maintaining accuracy, enabling longer-input transformer training.

**Why it matters:** Efficient attention and training techniques underpin the vision-language and imaging models the reader works with, where sequence length and compute are practical bottlenecks.


### 3. [Solar Open 2 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20062)
**Authors:** Sungrae Park, Sanghoon Kim, Gyoungjin Gim, Jungho Cho et al. (53 authors)  
**arXiv:** `2607.20062`  
**Tags:** Foundation Models, Mixture-of-Experts, Long-Context LLMs

**TL;DR:** Solar Open 2 is a 250B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts LLM with a 1M-token hybrid-attention window built for long-horizon agentic tasks.

**Problem:** Long-horizon agentic tasks require holding entire agent trajectories in context, which demands very long context windows without prohibitive attention cost.

**Method:** Solar Open 2 is a 250B-A15B MoE model scaled from Solar Open 1, reaching a 1M-token window via a hybrid attention stack that interleaves one softmax layer per three linear-attention layers, using no positional encoding and gating.

**Results:** The report presents a scaled MoE foundation model achieving 1M-token context suitable for full agent trajectories.

**Why it matters:** It documents current architectural techniques (MoE, hybrid/linear attention, long context) shaping foundation models the reader may build on or adapt.
