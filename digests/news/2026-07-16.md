### 1. [US intensifies attacks on Iran as Tehran hits back at Gulf states](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/16/us-intensifies-attacks-on-iran-as-tehran-hits-back-at-gulf-states)
*The Guardian · UK · left-leaning*

**What happened:** The US intensified its bombing campaign against Iran, striking targets near Tehran and disabling a tanker enforcing its naval blockade, while Iran retaliated with missiles against US bases in Gulf states. The sixth day of fighting threatens to escalate into all-out war and has collapsed the June US-Iran memorandum of understanding.

**Background:** Fighting reignited after the vaguely worded June 17 MoU over the Strait of Hormuz broke down, with the US reimposing a blockade on Iranian ports and Iran threatening to shut the strait.

**Why it matters:** A direct US-Iran war centered on the Strait of Hormuz threatens global energy supplies (diesel already above $5/gallon) and risks dragging in Gulf states, Iraq, Yemen's Houthis and Lebanon into a region-wide conflagration.

**Different views:** The Guardian frames it as Trump's chaotic, self-inflicted quagmire of U-turns; the Jerusalem Post frames US strikes as legally justified and strengthening Trump's escalation options while highlighting Iranian 'existential war' rhetoric; Al Jazeera emphasizes Iranian claims of strikes on US bases and a strike near an Ahvaz children's cancer hospital, foregrounding civilian harm.


### 2. [Israel-Lebanon talks conclude in Rome, pilot zones agreed, IDF may begin withdrawal in days](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-902625)
*The Jerusalem Post · Israeli · right-leaning*

**What happened:** Israel-Lebanon talks concluded in Rome with agreement on two pilot zones to test Lebanese sovereignty enforcement by the Lebanese armed forces, and the IDF may begin withdrawing within days. Separately, Lebanon's foreign minister said Beirut has decided to 'end Hezbollah's military presence.'

**Background:** The talks follow Trump-brokered efforts to stabilize Lebanon and cut Hezbollah's arms flow, with Syria reportedly cooperating against weapons smuggling after Hezbollah's degradation in the wider conflict.

**Why it matters:** A negotiated IDF withdrawal paired with Lebanese moves to disarm Hezbollah could mark a structural shift in the Israel-Lebanon front and Hezbollah's role in Lebanese politics.

**Different views:** The Jerusalem Post emphasizes Lebanese/Syrian cooperation against Hezbollah and a poll showing most Lebanese view its weapons as harmful; Al Jazeera foregrounds the human cost of Israeli strikes on Lebanese families (e.g. in Tyre), framing Israel as the aggressor.


### 3. [Israel blocking regional stability by refusing to withdraw, Syria's Sharaa tells Trump  - report](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-902557)
*The Jerusalem Post · Israeli · right-leaning*

**What happened:** Syrian leader Ahmed al-Sharaa told Trump that Israel is blocking regional stability by refusing to withdraw from Syrian territory, saying Israel would not include the word 'withdrawal' in any agreement and halted negotiations when the issue was raised. This comes amid US pressure on Israel to pull back from the buffer zone.

**Background:** Israel expanded into a Syrian buffer zone after Assad's fall, citing October 7 lessons about not letting hostile forces mass on its border; Trump is now pushing for an Israeli withdrawal as part of broader regional diplomacy.

**Why it matters:** The standoff pits US regional peace efforts against Israeli security doctrine and shapes the trajectory of post-Assad Syria's reintegration and Israel-Syria relations.

**Different views:** The Jerusalem Post presents competing Israeli-sourced analyses — one justifying continued presence via October 7 lessons, another conceding a rethink may be needed — while relaying Sharaa's accusation as a 'report,' keeping the Syrian framing at arm's length.
