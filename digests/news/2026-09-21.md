### 1. [Middle East braces for more violence as Iran claims US is planning to renew bombing campaign](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/20/middle-east-braces-for-more-violence-as-iran-claims-us-is-planning-to-renew-bombing-campaign)

**Background:** The US and Israel launched a war against Iran in late February; the fighting drew in Hezbollah, Iraqi Shia militias and the Houthis, and official Iranian data now show the oil and gas sector contracting 26 percent and GDP down roughly 10 percent.

### 2. [Inside Djibouti refugee camp for thousands fleeing Houthi advance in Yemen](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/c6z0z2nne11vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
*BBC Middle East · UK*

**What happened:** Thousands of Yemenis are fleeing a Houthi advance on Yemen's west coast, crossing the Bab al-Mandab Strait by boat to a refugee camp in Djibouti. The offensive comes alongside Houthi attacks on Riyadh that have drawn Saudi Arabia back toward open confrontation.

**Background:** The Houthis seized Sanaa in 2014 and fought a years-long war against a Saudi-led coalition; a fragile lull has now collapsed as the group joined the fighting on Iran's side.

**Why it matters:** A reignited Yemeni civil war threatens one of the world's busiest shipping chokepoints and could pull Saudi Arabia back into a conflict it spent years trying to exit.

**Different views:** The BBC reports from inside the Djibouti camp and centres displaced civilians without assigning blame; the Guardian's commentary traces the offensive directly to Trump's Iran war; the Jerusalem Post treats the Houthi threat mainly as a shared Israeli-Saudi security problem and asks how far Riyadh will coordinate with Israel.


### 3. [Israeli attacks on Gaza kill three, including a child](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/21/israeli-attacks-on-gaza-kill-three-including-a-child?traffic_source=rss)

**Background:** The war in Gaza is approaching its fourth year, and the October ceasefire has not halted strikes or the deepening humanitarian collapse for people living in tents among the ruins.

**Why it matters:** Continued killings under a nominal ceasefire undercut the agreement's credibility and complicate any diplomacy around Palestinian statehood at the UN this week.

**Different views:** Al Jazeera puts the ceasefire in scare quotes and counts Palestinian casualties as the lead fact; the Guardian conveys the same reality through the daily life of a pregnant woman whose three children were killed; Jerusalem Post coverage of Gaza this cycle centres instead on Israeli domestic accusations over aid funding and on the US decision to bar Abbas from the UN, with the strike deaths largely absent.
