### 2. [Former Emir of Qatar Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani dies aged 74](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cql1qdq14xro?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
*BBC Middle East · UK · mainstream*

**What happened:** Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani, the former Emir of Qatar, died aged 74. He took power in 1996 in a bloodless coup against his father and transformed Qatar into a gas-rich regional power, founding Al Jazeera and advancing women's reforms during his 18-year rule.

**Background:** Sheikh Hamad ruled until abdicating in 2013 in favor of his son; he reoriented Qatar's foreign policy and media influence, making it a diplomatic heavyweight punching above its size.

**Different views:** Al Jazeera, which he founded, runs an affectionate farewell emphasizing women's empowerment; BBC notes the bloodless coup and gas-driven transformation neutrally; Israeli JPost analyzes how he 'reshaped the modern Middle East' from a more strategic, arm's-length vantage.


### 3. [UK to list Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps as terrorist organisation](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/jul/13/uk-list-iran-islamic-revolutionary-guard-corps-irgc-terrorist-organisation)
*The Guardian · UK · left-leaning*

**What happened:** The UK announced it will list Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) as a terrorist organization, with the Home Office banning support for the group. The move follows IRGC links to death threats and intimidation on British soil.

**Background:** The proscription is a major escalation of diplomatic tensions with Tehran and comes amid the broader US-Iran military confrontation in the Gulf.

**Why it matters:** Designating a branch of a state's official military as a terrorist organization is a significant diplomatic step that further isolates Iran and could trigger retaliation or a rupture in UK-Iran relations.

**Different views:** BBC and the Guardian cover it similarly as a domestic-security-driven escalation; framing across outlets is largely consistent, with the Guardian stressing the diplomatic rupture with Tehran.
