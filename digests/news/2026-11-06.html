3. Russia agreed to supply Iran with 90 helicopter missile launchers

The Guardian · UK

The same day, the US sanctioned three individuals for aiding Iran's air defences.

Background: Russia-Iran military ties have been growing throughout the broader conflict period, with Iran previously supplying Russia with drones for use in Ukraine. The arms deal comes as Iran simultaneously pursues diplomatic negotiations with the US over its nuclear programme.

Why it matters: The deal illustrates that Iran is pursuing a dual-track strategy — diplomacy with the US while hardening its military position with Russian support — complicating US and Israeli calculations about the costs of continued or escalated conflict.

Different views: The Guardian, working from the leaked documents, presents the arms deal in detail and foregrounds it as evidence of the depth of Russia-Iran military cooperation, with the Ukrainian intelligence firm as the source. The Jerusalem Post pairs the arms story with the US sanctions response, framing the two together as part of an adversarial dynamic and emphasising the threat the deal poses to US and Israeli security interests.
