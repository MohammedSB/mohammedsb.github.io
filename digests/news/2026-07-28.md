### 3. [Asia ‘scraping the bottom of the barrel’ as Red Sea oil blockade worsens energy crisis](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/28/asia-energy-oil-crisis-red-sea-blockade-houthis)

**Background:** The Houthis have been escalating attacks on Saudi oil infrastructure and commercial shipping in solidarity with Iran, mirroring Tehran's pressure tactics in the Strait of Hormuz. **Why it matters:** The simultaneous closure or threat to two of the world's most critical oil shipping chokepoints represents an unprecedented energy security challenge for Asia and global markets, with cascading economic consequences far beyond the immediate conflict zone.
