### 1. [US-Iran talks continue, but ‘deal unlikely’ before midterm elections](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/9/29/us-iran-talks-continue-but-deal-unlikely-before-midterm-elections?traffic_source=rss)

**Different views:** Al Jazeera foregrounds Iran's leverage — its Hormuz stranglehold and the concessions it is extracting — framing Iran as a rational actor seeking relief for allies like the Houthis. Trump's denial of sanctions offers is reported neutrally by the Post but its broader framing treats any Iranian compromise as something to be extracted rather than negotiated.


### 2. [Iran suspected in bomb plot against RAF base used by US Air Force, UK police investigating - report](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-909919)
*The Jerusalem Post · Israeli*

**What happened:** UK police are investigating a suspected bomb plot against RAF Fairford, a base also used by the US Air Force, after five men with three vans were found near the perimeter in the early hours of Sunday. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio called it 'a very serious situation' involving a 'foreign actor.' All five were released on bail without immediate charge.

**Background:** RAF Fairford is a strategically significant base that has historically hosted US B-52 bombers and other heavy assets. Iran has been accused of escalating covert operations targeting Western infrastructure amid the ongoing US-Iran conflict.

**Why it matters:** If Iranian involvement is confirmed, it would mark a significant escalation of Iran's campaign against Western targets on European soil, with major implications for NATO allies and the diplomatic track of US-Iran talks.

**Different views:** The Jerusalem Post leads with Iran as the suspected actor and frames the plot within a pattern of Iranian aggression. The Guardian is more cautious, noting Iran's 'mixed track record' on such plots, that the men were released on bail, and that investigators are far from determining the cause — foregrounding legal uncertainty over Iranian culpability. Al Jazeera reports Rubio's 'foreign actor' framing without attributing it to Iran, keeping attribution open.
