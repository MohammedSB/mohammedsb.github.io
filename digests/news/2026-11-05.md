### 3. [Four oil tankers destroyed by naval mines in Strait of Hormuz, IRGC announces](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-913786)
*The Jerusalem Post · Israeli*

**What happened:** Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps announced that four oil tankers were destroyed by naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, with two additional vessels seized. The IRGC said the tankers were still ablaze at the time of the announcement, and a separate report confirmed two tankers and a tugboat caught fire in the same waterway.

**Background:** The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical maritime chokepoints, through which roughly a fifth of global oil supply passes. Iran has historically threatened and occasionally acted to disrupt traffic there during periods of heightened tension with the US and its Gulf allies.

**Why it matters:** Destruction of multiple tankers and seizure of vessels in the Strait of Hormuz represents a major escalation with immediate consequences for global energy markets, Gulf state security, and the credibility of US naval deterrence in the region.

**Different views:** The Jerusalem Post reports the IRGC's announcement factually and in detail, treating it as a serious military escalation and linking it to broader concerns about Iranian aggression and nuclear ambitions. Al Jazeera and the Guardian do not appear to have dedicated articles on this specific incident in this batch, leaving the Jerusalem Post as the primary source — meaning the story is currently framed almost exclusively through an Israeli commercial outlet with a right-leaning editorial orientation, without Arab or Western liberal counterweights present.
