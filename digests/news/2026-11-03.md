### 3. [Trump’s troubles with Iran could open the door to China](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/03/trump-iran-middle-east-china-gulf-hormuz-xi-jinping)
*The Guardian · UK*

**What happened:** China has been conducting a flurry of high-level diplomatic meetings with Gulf leaders, seeking to deepen its influence in the Middle East as the US-Iran conflict creates uncertainty about American reliability as a regional partner. The diplomatic push comes ahead of an anticipated Trump-Xi meeting at the APEC conference.

**Background:** China brokered the landmark 2023 Saudi-Iran rapprochement and has steadily expanded its economic ties across the Gulf. The ongoing US-Iran war has disrupted energy flows through the Strait of Hormuz, a chokepoint for roughly 20% of global oil supplies, giving Beijing additional leverage as Gulf states seek alternative partnerships.

**Why it matters:** A sustained Chinese diplomatic offensive in the Gulf could accelerate a long-term shift in regional alignment away from exclusive US security dependence, with significant consequences for global energy markets, US strategic posture, and the future architecture of Middle East diplomacy.
