### 1. [Why is Trump threatening to strike Iran’s Pickaxe Mountain again, now?](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/5/why-is-trump-threatening-to-strike-irans-pickaxe-mountain-again-now?traffic_source=rss)
*Al Jazeera English · Qatar · pro-Palestinian*

**What happened:** Trump has publicly threatened new US strikes on Pickaxe Mountain, the deeply buried tunnel complex near Iran's damaged Natanz enrichment site, after an oil tanker was hit off Kharg Island. The threat comes alongside reports that Washington is weighing follow-on military options while Iranian leaders debate escalation, and that the US and allies want the IAEA board to refer Iran to the UN Security Council.

**Background:** Israel began bombing Iranian nuclear sites in June 2025 and the US subsequently joined strikes; Pickaxe Mountain is one of the few parts of Iran's nuclear infrastructure believed to have survived largely intact.

**Why it matters:** Striking a fortified underground site would mark a significant widening of the war and risks Iranian retaliation against shipping and US forces, with the Strait of Hormuz and global oil supply directly exposed.

**Different views:** Al Jazeera frames the threat as unprovoked escalation against what remains of Iran's civilian nuclear programme and questions Trump's timing; the Jerusalem Post presents it as necessary pressure on a defiant Tehran, emphasising Iranian confidence and reluctance to end the war, while the BBC treats it neutrally as an open question of whether either side has an exit strategy.


### 2. [Israelis shoot dead two Palestinian teenagers after settlers encircle village](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/03/israelis-shoot-dead-two-palestinian-teenagers-after-settlers-encircle-village)
*The Guardian · UK · left-leaning*

**What happened:** Israeli forces shot dead two Palestinian teenagers, Khalil Abu Alia and Omar al-Naasan, in al-Mughayyir after settlers encircled the village under military escort, and troops razed an entire village outside Hebron. The killings came as US Ambassador Mike Huckabee visited Turmus Ayya and said Israel must protect West Bank Palestinians, labelling some violent settlers 'terrorists'.

**Background:** Settler attacks and army raids in the occupied West Bank have surged since October 2023, with repeated displacement of Palestinian herding communities and rare accountability for perpetrators.

**Why it matters:** The combination of teenage deaths, village demolition and an unusually blunt rebuke from a strongly pro-Israel US ambassador points to mounting international friction over the West Bank even as attention stays on Gaza and Iran.

**Different views:** The Guardian describes an ongoing campaign of 'Israeli terror and ethnic cleansing'; the BBC centres a bereaved father's testimony without adopting that characterisation; Al Jazeera highlights Huckabee's visit as largely 'symbolic' according to analysts, while Israeli coverage of settler violence is comparatively muted and framed around security operations.


### 3. [Yemen government launches own air attacks on Houthis: What that means](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/5/yemen-government-launches-own-air-attacks-on-houthis-what-that-means?traffic_source=rss)
*Al Jazeera English · Qatar · pro-Palestinian*

**What happened:** A major Houthi offensive toward the Bab al-Mandeb strait has triggered some of Yemen's worst fighting in years, with intense clashes across Taiz and Hodeidah and rapid civilian displacement. Yemen's internationally recognised government has responded with its own air attacks on Houthi positions, and a Houthi missile strike on a crowded road near Taiz killed at least four civilians.

**Background:** Yemen's front lines had been largely frozen since a 2022 truce; the Houthis are now pushing toward the port of Mocha, less than 50 miles from the strait that carries a large share of global shipping.

**Why it matters:** Houthi control of Mocha and the Bab al-Mandeb would give Tehran's closest remaining regional ally a chokehold on Red Sea traffic at the same moment the US and Iran are trading strikes.

**Different views:** Al Jazeera emphasises the civilian toll and the significance of the Yemeni government developing independent air capability; the Jerusalem Post frames the offensive primarily as an Iranian-backed threat to maritime chokepoints and Israeli shipping interests.
