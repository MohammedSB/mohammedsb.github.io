### 3. [Syria forms first post-Assad parliament to kickstart new era of 'managed democracy'](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-901197)
*The Jerusalem Post · Israeli · right-leaning*

**What happened:** Syria formed its first post-Assad parliament, with President Sharaa naming the final 70 appointed lawmakers to sit alongside 140 elected seats in a 210-member chamber that will hold limited power under a presidential system. Only fifteen women were included, and the body is being described as 'managed democracy.'

**Background:** The assembly follows the fall of Bashar al-Assad and Sharaa's consolidation of power, with two-thirds of seats chosen by regional electoral colleges.

**Why it matters:** It marks a pivotal institutional step in Syria's transition, but its constrained authority raises questions about how genuine the country's democratization will be.

**Different views:** BBC highlights the low representation of women and the electoral college mechanics as a mainstream institutional story, while Jerusalem Post frames it skeptically as 'managed democracy' and cites polling showing low Syrian confidence and demands for Damascus-Sweida talks.
