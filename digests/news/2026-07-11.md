### 2. [US wants Iran to pledge to stop shooting at ships in Strait of Hormuz](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crelyq79x71o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
*BBC Middle East · UK · mainstream*

**What happened:** US officials, including VP JD Vance, are joining talks resuming in Oman where Washington demands Iran publicly pledge to stop attacking shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. Europe is separately weighing proposals to allow non-compulsory navigational fees in the strait, while a UN shipping agency urged countries to reject Iran's claim of sovereignty over the waterway.

**Background:** Iran attributed recent attacks on shipping to 'an errant part of their system' as the vital oil-transit chokepoint became a flashpoint in the wider US-Iran confrontation.

**Why it matters:** The Strait of Hormuz carries a large share of global oil; disruption or Iranian control threatens energy markets and international freedom of navigation.

**Different views:** Jerusalem Post and the UN framing stress rejecting Iranian 'control' and sovereignty claims, the Guardian focuses on European toll-and-diplomacy mechanics, and coverage differs on whether Iran's attacks were deliberate policy or a rogue malfunction.

*The Jerusalem Post · Israeli · right-leaning*
