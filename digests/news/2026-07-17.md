### 1. [US intensifies attacks on Iran as Tehran hits back at Gulf states](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/16/us-intensifies-attacks-on-iran-as-tehran-hits-back-at-gulf-states)
*The Guardian · UK · left-leaning*

**What happened:** The US intensified a sixth day of air and naval strikes on Iran, hitting bridges, energy facilities, a southern port and targets near Tehran, and firing on tankers to enforce a blockade of Iranian ports in the Strait of Hormuz. Iran retaliated with missiles and drones against US allies and bases in the Gulf, with the IRGC claiming hits in Kuwait, Jordan, Bahrain and Syria and threatening 'more crushing' attacks.

**Background:** The fighting jeopardizes a June US-Iran memorandum of understanding, and marks a sharp escalation over shipping tolls and control of the Strait of Hormuz, through which much of the world's oil flows.

**Why it matters:** A drift toward open regional war threatens global energy supplies (diesel above $5/gallon), draws in Gulf states hosting 50,000+ US troops, and could reshape the balance of power across the Middle East.

**Different views:** Al Jazeera stresses US strikes on 'civilian infrastructure' (Bushehr, hospitals, bridges) and Iranian casualties; the Jerusalem Post/CENTCOM frame the campaign as 'holding Iran accountable' by hitting military targets; BBC and the Guardian foreground the risk of all-out war and Trump's ambivalence about whether to 'finish off' Iran.


### 2. [Iran reports fresh strikes near Qeshm Island and accuses US over ‘barbaric’ hospital attack – as it happened](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/jul/16/iran-us-donald-trump-war-strait-hormuz-oil-israel-lebanon-latest-news-updates)

**What happened:** Lebanon's foreign minister Youssef Raggi announced the government has decided to 'end Hezbollah's military presence,' asserting that decisions on war and foreign policy are the exclusive prerogative of the Lebanese state. **Background:** Hezbollah is one of the Middle East's most heavily armed non-state militias; the disarmament drive follows the fall of the Assad regime and a US-backed effort to stabilize Lebanon.

**Different views:** The Jerusalem Post frames Syria's interdiction of arms and Lebanon's move as showcasing pro-Western regional commitment and a win for Trump's peace push; Al Jazeera emphasizes the security-vacuum risk and European calls to backfill UNIFIL.

**Background:** The dissolution ends a full parliamentary term and comes as regional war with Iran and questions over Gaza policy dominate Israeli politics.

**Why it matters:** The vote will shape Israeli policy on Iran, Gaza reconstruction and potential Saudi normalization at a moment of acute regional crisis; fears exist Netanyahu could gamble on a new Gaza offensive before polling day.
