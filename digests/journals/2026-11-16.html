1. Clinical-SAM: Agentic Test-Time Adaptation of SAM via Physician-in-the-Loop for Clinical Tumor Segmentation.

IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging  ·  Nov 12, 2026

Nourhan Bayasi, Jamil Fayyad, Fereshteh Yousefirizi, Arman Rahmim

Foundation ModelsMedical Image SegmentationHuman-in-the-Loop

Segmentation foundation models like SAM often perform worse when deployed at new hospitals, and current test-time adaptation methods either remove the physician or add a heavy annotation burden. Clinical-SAM proposes an agentic, physician-in-the-loop test-time adaptation of SAM for tumor segmentation, aiming to keep clinical grounding while reducing how much physicians have to annotate.

2. CPCLNet: Advancing Cross-Modal Few-Shot Medical Image Segmentation with Clustering-Inspired Prototype Consistency Learning.

IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging  ·  Nov 12, 2026

Peng Zhang, Chen Liu, Yida Wu, Heyang Zhao et al. (8 authors)

Few-Shot LearningMedical Image SegmentationCross-Modal Learning

Cross-modal few-shot medical image segmentation tries to segment targets in a new imaging modality from a few labeled examples, but differences between modalities corrupt the prototypes and similarity maps. CPCLNet uses clustering-inspired prototype consistency learning to make support-query matching more robust and improve segmentation in the target modality.

4. Foundation artificial intelligence models for health recognition by use of face photographs (FAHR-Face): a model development and validation study.

Lancet Digital Health  ·  Jun 16, 2027

Fridolin Haugg, Grace Lee, John He, Leonard Nürnberg et al. (17 authors)

FAHR-Face is a foundation model trained on more than 40 million facial photographs and then fine-tuned to estimate biological age and predict survival. It shows how large-scale pretraining on non-invasive images can be adapted to several downstream health-prediction tasks.

8. Multi-phase liver CT virtual contrast enhancement and diagnosis based on phase-aware diffusion network.

IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging  ·  Nov 10, 2026

Ziang Di, Wei Zhao, Rongjun Ge, Zhan Wu et al. (8 authors)

Generative ModelsDiffusion ModelsMedical Imaging

A phase-aware diffusion network generates virtual multi-phase contrast-enhanced liver CT from non-contrast CT, which could reduce radiation exposure and remove the need for contrast agents. The synthesized images are also used for liver tumor diagnosis, so the paper addresses both image generation quality and clinical usefulness.
