### 1. [Bridging Brain Connectome and Clinical Notes: A Novel Multimodal Framework for MCI Study.](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2026.3755232)
*IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging · Nov 19, 2026*  
**Authors:** Jing Zhang, Li Su, Rong Zhang, Tianming Liu et al. (5 authors)  
**Tags:** Multimodal Learning, Neuroimaging, Clinical Text

This paper presents a framework that combines brain connectome data from imaging with clinical notes to help diagnose mild cognitive impairment. It addresses two problems: encoding brain structure and function so that they reflect meaningful neurological patterns, and aligning that imaging information with text written by clinicians.

### 2. [Foundation artificial intelligence models for health recognition by use of face photographs (FAHR-Face): a model development and validation study.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.landig.2026.101056)
*Lancet Digital Health · Jun 16, 2027*  
**Authors:** Fridolin Haugg, Grace Lee, John He, Leonard Nürnberg et al. (17 authors)  
**Tags:** Foundation Models, Computer Vision, Clinical AI

FAHR-Face is a foundation model trained on more than 40 million facial photographs and then fine-tuned for two tasks: estimating biological age and predicting survival. It suggests that large-scale pretraining on ordinary photos can produce non-invasive health biomarkers.

### 3. [Multimodal CT-pathology imaging deep learning model for risk stratification in resected stage I lung adenocarcinoma](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03467-8)
*npj Digital Medicine · Nov 23, 2026*  
**Authors:** Jialiang Wen, Yanrui Jin, Yuzhou Wang, Xinchen Shen et al. (21 authors)  
**Tags:** Multimodal Learning, Medical Imaging, Computational Pathology

A deep learning model combines CT scans with pathology images to sort patients with resected stage I lung adenocarcinoma by their risk of recurrence. Fusing radiology and histology aims to identify high-risk early-stage patients better than either modality alone.

### 4. [Ordinal conformal partial-label prediction for medical image grading.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2026.104399)
*Medical Image Analysis · Nov 19, 2026*  
**Authors:** Lie Ju, Peilun Shi, Yiqun Lin, Siyuan Yan et al. (7 authors)  
**Tags:** Medical Imaging, Uncertainty Quantification, Conformal Prediction

Grading medical images is often ambiguous between neighbouring severity levels, so this method turns partial-label classifier outputs into continuous ranges of grades instead of a single answer. It uses conformal prediction so that those ranges come with statistical coverage guarantees and respect the order of the grades.

### 5. [ECG foundation models for cardiovascular screening in a cohort of young competitive athletes](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03474-9)
*npj Digital Medicine · Nov 21, 2026*  
**Authors:** Christian Goelz, Simon Lang, Jenny Schlichtiger, Stefan Brunner et al. (5 authors)  
**Tags:** Foundation Models, Cardiology AI, Screening

This study tests ECG foundation models for cardiovascular screening in a cohort of young competitive athletes, a group where telling normal athletic heart changes apart from disease is hard. It shows how well pretrained physiological-signal models transfer to a specialised clinical population.

### 6. [Bias and equity in large language model-based applications for health care: a scoping review.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.landig.2026.101112)
*Lancet Digital Health · Nov 18, 2026*  
**Authors:** Jingchi Liao, Huitao Li, Rui Yang, Sabarinath Vinod Nair et al. (18 authors)  
**Tags:** LLM Evaluation, Fairness, Clinical AI

This scoping review maps the research on bias and equity in large language model applications for health care. It finds that concerns are widely discussed but rarely studied systematically or turned into concrete mitigation, and it highlights gaps for future evaluation work.

### 7. [DAF: Deterministic affinity-guided latent flow for flexible many-to-many multiphase liver MRI synthesis.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2026.104402)
*Medical Image Analysis · Nov 20, 2026*  
**Authors:** Shunjie Dong, Yulin Pu, Zhehan Shen, Xueyang Zou et al. (9 authors)  
**Tags:** Generative Models, Medical Image Synthesis, MRI

DAF is a latent flow model that generates multiphase contrast-enhanced liver MRI from other phases, including non-contrast scans, in a flexible many-to-many way. The goal is to reduce dependence on gadolinium contrast agents and shorten scan times while keeping the images useful for diagnosing liver lesions.

### 8. [Multimodal multitask deep learning for predicting tertiary lymphoid structures and peritoneal recurrence in gastric cancer: a multicentre development, validation, and testing study.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.landig.2026.101075)
*Lancet Digital Health · Nov 19, 2026*  
**Authors:** Zihan Li, Taojun Zhang, Wei Wang, Xiangde Luo et al. (19 authors)  
**Tags:** Multimodal Learning, Medical Imaging, Oncology AI

A multimodal, multitask deep learning model predicts tertiary lymphoid structures, which are markers of immune response, and the risk of peritoneal recurrence after gastric cancer surgery. Developed and tested across several centres, it offers a non-invasive alternative to invasive tissue assessment for stratifying patients.
