4. Cortex-grounded diffusion models for brain image generation.

Medical Image Analysis  ·  Sep 29, 2026

Fabian Bongratz, Yitong Li, Sama Elbaroudy, Timo Grimmer et al. (7 authors)

Diffusion ModelsSynthetic DataNeuroimaging

The authors propose diffusion models that use cortical anatomy to condition the generation of synthetic brain images, instead of weaker signals such as labels or text. The goal is anatomically realistic synthetic data that can make up for rare phenotypes, differences between scanners, and limited longitudinal coverage.
