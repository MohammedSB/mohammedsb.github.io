### 1. [PIPA: Prior-Driven Prompting with Diagnosis-Oriented Retrieval-Augmentation for 3D Radiology Report Generation.](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2026.3710717)
*IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging · Jul 7, 2026*  
**Authors:** Qiushi Yang, Wuyang Li, Xiaoqing Guo, Maymay Cerys Harwood et al. (9 authors)  
**Tags:** Radiology Report Generation, Vision-Language Models, Medical Imaging

PIPA generates radiology reports from 3D volumes by adding prior-driven prompting and diagnosis-oriented retrieval-augmentation, addressing the large background regions and volume-specific challenges that generic multimodal models ignore. This directly targets automatic 3D report generation to assist clinical reporting and reduce radiologist workload.

### 6. [UniTransAD: Unified Translation Framework for Anomaly Detection in Brain MRI.](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2026.3711975)
*IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging · Jul 10, 2026*  
**Authors:** Qi Zhang, Xia Li, Yibo Hu, Jianqi Sun  
**Tags:** Medical Imaging, Anomaly Detection, Generative Models

UniTransAD is a unified image-translation framework for unsupervised anomaly detection in brain MRI that generalizes across diseases, sequences, and missing-data scenarios. It aims to catch subtle anomalies that reconstruction-based methods miss while staying flexible to varying input sequences.

### 7. [Controllable synthesis of dermoscopic images using diffusion models for enhanced computer aided diagnosis and detection.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2026.104191)
*Medical Image Analysis · Jul 8, 2026*  
**Authors:** Junjie Shentu, Matthew Watson, Noura Al Moubayed  
**Tags:** Generative Models, Medical Imaging, Data Augmentation

DiDGen uses text-to-image diffusion models to controllably synthesize high-quality dermoscopic images, countering limited and imbalanced skin-lesion datasets. The generated data augments training and improves downstream computer-aided diagnosis performance.
