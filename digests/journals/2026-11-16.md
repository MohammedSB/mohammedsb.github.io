### 1. [Clinical-SAM: Agentic Test-Time Adaptation of SAM via Physician-in-the-Loop for Clinical Tumor Segmentation.](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2026.3753143)
*IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging · Nov 12, 2026*  
**Authors:** Nourhan Bayasi, Jamil Fayyad, Fereshteh Yousefirizi, Arman Rahmim  
**Tags:** Foundation Models, Medical Image Segmentation, Human-in-the-Loop

Segmentation foundation models like SAM often perform worse when deployed at new hospitals, and current test-time adaptation methods either remove the physician or add a heavy annotation burden. Clinical-SAM proposes an agentic, physician-in-the-loop test-time adaptation of SAM for tumor segmentation, aiming to keep clinical grounding while reducing how much physicians have to annotate.

### 2. [CPCLNet: Advancing Cross-Modal Few-Shot Medical Image Segmentation with Clustering-Inspired Prototype Consistency Learning.](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2026.3753036)
*IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging · Nov 12, 2026*  
**Authors:** Peng Zhang, Chen Liu, Yida Wu, Heyang Zhao et al. (8 authors)  
**Tags:** Few-Shot Learning, Medical Image Segmentation, Cross-Modal Learning

Cross-modal few-shot medical image segmentation tries to segment targets in a new imaging modality from a few labeled examples, but differences between modalities corrupt the prototypes and similarity maps. CPCLNet uses clustering-inspired prototype consistency learning to make support-query matching more robust and improve segmentation in the target modality.

### 4. [Foundation artificial intelligence models for health recognition by use of face photographs (FAHR-Face): a model development and validation study.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.landig.2026.101056)
*Lancet Digital Health · Jun 16, 2027*  
**Authors:** Fridolin Haugg, Grace Lee, John He, Leonard Nürnberg et al. (17 authors)  
**Tags:** Foundation Models, Self-Supervised Learning, Health Prediction

FAHR-Face is a foundation model trained on more than 40 million facial photographs and then fine-tuned to estimate biological age and predict survival. It shows how large-scale pretraining on non-invasive images can be adapted to several downstream health-prediction tasks.

### 6. [Learning uncertainty fields for joint brain stroke diagnosis and intrinsic lesion localization in CT and MRI](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03458-9)
*npj Digital Medicine · Nov 14, 2026*  
**Authors:** Zehao Jin, Shuzhen Xu, Xiaojun Dong, Jieyi Yang et al. (7 authors)  
**Tags:** Medical Imaging, Uncertainty Estimation, Weakly Supervised Localization

The authors propose learning uncertainty fields that both diagnose brain stroke and localize lesions directly within the model, across both CT and MRI. The goal is to provide interpretable lesion localization alongside the diagnosis without separate segmentation labels.

### 7. [Measurement fidelity and external validation of automated breast DCE-MRI segmentation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03446-z)
*npj Digital Medicine · Nov 14, 2026*  
**Authors:** Chunlei Tan, Li Chen, Jinling Xu, Jian Zheng et al. (6 authors)  
**Tags:** Medical Image Segmentation, External Validation, Breast MRI

This study evaluates how accurately automated breast DCE-MRI segmentation tools reproduce clinically meaningful measurements, and tests them on external data. It highlights the gap between good segmentation overlap scores and reliable downstream measurements.

### 8. [Multi-phase liver CT virtual contrast enhancement and diagnosis based on phase-aware diffusion network.](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2026.3752023)
*IEEE Trans. Medical Imaging · Nov 10, 2026*  
**Authors:** Ziang Di, Wei Zhao, Rongjun Ge, Zhan Wu et al. (8 authors)  
**Tags:** Generative Models, Diffusion Models, Medical Imaging

A phase-aware diffusion network generates virtual multi-phase contrast-enhanced liver CT from non-contrast CT, which could reduce radiation exposure and remove the need for contrast agents. The synthesized images are also used for liver tumor diagnosis, so the paper addresses both image generation quality and clinical usefulness.
