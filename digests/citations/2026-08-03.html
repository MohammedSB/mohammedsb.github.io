Do Medical Foundation Models Generalize on the African Brain?

Kaouther Mouheb, Gonzalo Esteban Mosquera Rojas, J. V. Leeuwen, Stefan Klein et al. (5 authors) · 2026

What they say: MedSAM2 is characterized as a segmentation-specific medical foundation model — one trained directly for the segmentation task, in contrast to task-independent self-supervised feature extractors like 3DINO — and it is one of four FMs benchmarked (alongside BrainIAC, 3DINO, and Medical-SAM2) against a from-scratch baseline for generalization to African brain MRI cohorts, specifically tumor segmentation on BraTS-Africa.

  • Currently, available FMs can be broadly divided into two categories: those trained specifically for segmentation, e.g. Med-SAM2 [9], and those trained as task-independent feature extractors with self-supervision, such as 3DINO [18].
  • These comprise two generalist FMs, BrainIAC [13] and 3DINO [18], and two segmentation-specific FMs, MedSAM2 [9] and Medical-SAM2 [19].
