Citing: MedSAM2: Segment Anything in 3D Medical Images and Videos

Confidence-Aware Teacher-Student Distillation for 3D Medical Segmentation

Georgios Triantafyllou, D. Iakovidis · 2026

What they say: MedSAM2 is characterized as a domain-specific medical adaptation of SAM2 that carries promptable segmentation into video and 3D volumetric data, and it is adopted directly as the frozen foundation-model teacher in the proposed framework — taking point prompts on a single 2D slice and propagating them into full-volume pseudo-annotations for training the student.

  • Building upon this, next-generation architectures, such as SAM2 [13] and its domain-specific variants, such as Med-SAM2 [11] have recently extended these capabilities to video and 3D volumetric data.
  • MedSAM2 is used as the frozen foundation-model teacher [11].
