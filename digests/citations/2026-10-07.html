What they say: 3DReasonKnee (Sambara et al., 2026) is cited, alongside UniBiomed, as a medical benchmark that requires diagnosis and localization in a single pass but scores the two separately. It is also cited as precedent for the closed-set formulation of grounded diagnosis that AgroGround copies in its recognition-and-localization instructions: the model gets four candidate targets, must pick the one that is present, and must localize its evidence.

  • AgriDoctor (Zhang et al., 2026) and AgriMM (Wang & Liu, 2026) carry manual boxes, but in neither does language select the target, and language-conditioned agricultural grounding data exists for pests (Yang et al., 2026b), not disease.
  • UniBiomed (Wu et al., 2026) and 3DReasonKnee (Sambara et al., 2026) require diagnosis and localization in one pass but score them separately, and XBench (Luo et al., 2026) finds recognition-strong, grounding-weak VLMs.
