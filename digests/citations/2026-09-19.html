Citing: CRIMSON: A Clinically-Grounded LLM-Based Metric for Generative Radiology Report Evaluation

Reporting Practice Matters: The Impact of Reference Choice on Chest X-ray Report Evaluation

Daniel P. Jeong, Charles Q. Li, H. Hosseiny, Nitya M. Bhalla et al. (8 authors) · 2026

What they say: CRIMSON is treated as one of the established, clinically-validated LLM-judge metrics that correlate well with radiologist judgment (alongside GREEN, RadCliQ-v1, RaTEScore, and RadGraph-F1), and is evaluated using the authors' open-source MedGemma-4B-based implementation, with scores on a [-1,1] range rather than the [0,1] range of most other metrics. In the sensitivity analysis it emerges as the most stable metric overall to variation in reference reporting style (mean IQR 0.083), though it still varies noticeably along certain axes (max IQR 0.345), such as minimal Findings Scope and Section Assignment — and the paper notes that, being LLM-judge-based, its observed trends could change with a different backbone LLM.

  • All metrics range between 0 and 1, except CRIMSON (range: [-1,1]) and RadCliQ-v1 (range: (0, ∞ )).
  • As LLM judge metrics (CRIMSON, GREEN) can be implemented with any LLM of choice, the specific trends observed in our study may change when a different model is used.
  • For CRIMSON and GREEN, we use the open-source implementations based on MedGemma-4B (Sellergren et al., 2025) and Llama-2-7B (Touvron et al., 2023), respectively, as provided by the authors.

Citing: MedSAM2: Segment Anything in 3D Medical Images and Videos

Automated workflow for target volume propagation in online adaptive magnetic resonance guided radiotherapy for lung lesions using foundation models

D. Dudás, T. Blöcker, Miguel Palacios, Cheng-Tao Wei et al. (9 authors) · 2026

What they say: MedSAM2 is used as one of two publicly available promptable segmentation foundation models (alongside nnInteractive) to refine registration-propagated GTV contours on daily MRI in an automated online adaptive MRI-guided radiotherapy workflow for lung lesions.

  • In this study, two publicly available segmentation foundation models were investigated - MedSAM2 and nnInteractive [22,23].
