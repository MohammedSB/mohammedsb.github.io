Citing: MedSAM2: Segment Anything in 3D Medical Images and Videos

MedSAM2-Anatomy: Training-Free Inference-Time Optimization for Musculoskeletal Segmentation

J. G. Henao, Nicholas Bünger, Benedikt Herzog, Cindy Guerrero Toro et al. (14 authors) · 2026

What they say: MedSAM2 is characterized as a promptable 3D medical foundation model — a SAM2 medical adaptation that generalizes across modalities and anatomies without task-specific training but requires manual prompting — and is used frozen in MedSAM2-Anatomy, where 2D mask and bounding-box prompts extracted from TotalSegmentator's expanded anatomical prior at seed slices are fed to it so its bidirectional memory attention propagates each segmentation through the volume, yielding N candidate masks per anatomy. It also serves as one of the two standalone baselines (alongside TotalSegmentator), since both are the framework's frozen components evaluated on their own.
