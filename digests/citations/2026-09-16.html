Citing: Segment Anything in Medical Images and Videos: Benchmark and Deployment

IMVS: Interactive Medical Volume Segmentation with Test-Time Adaptation - A New Method for Annotating Radiology Datasets

Abhilaksh Singh Reen, Kushal Borkar, Ritvik Mahapatra · 2026

What they say: Grouped with MedSAM as a SAM-derived medical foundation model: cited as evidence that such models generalize across many segmentation targets but lose accuracy under medical distribution shift, and that they are typically run per-slice on 3D volumes rather than natively in 3D. On that basis it is positioned as ignoring inter-slice continuity and thus interaction-inefficient for volumetric annotation, in contrast to volume-level approaches like PRISM and nnInteractive.

  • Foundation models (SAM [6], MedSAM [13, 14]) generalize across targets but degrade under medical distribution shift and are typically applied per-slice; lighter 3D approaches such as PRISM [8] and promptable systems like nnInteractive [5] address volumes more directly.
