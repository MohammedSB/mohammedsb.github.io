Citing: ReXGroundingCT: A 3D Chest CT Dataset for Segmentation of Findings from Free-Text Reports

What they say: ReXGroundingCT is used as the main benchmark and as the base for the new ReXGroundingCTv2, which adds 1134 training scans and 1895 findings. CTLink localized 82% of findings (hit rate 0.816) on its 200-scan validation set. ReXGroundingCT also supplies the inter-rater reference: on 50 test scans segmented by two independent radiologists, the second reader reached a hit rate of only 0.41 for non-focal findings versus 0.84 for focal findings, and CTLink matches or exceeds that agreement.
