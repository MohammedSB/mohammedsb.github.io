Citing: MedSAM2: Segment Anything in 3D Medical Images and Videos

A4-MR: Abnormality-aware Anatomical Alignment for MR

Noel DSouza, Zhi-Jian Yang, Nishank Singla, Pranay Doshi et al. (17 authors) · 2026

What they say: MedSAM2 is described as a promptable medical segmentation foundation model that takes 2D box prompts and propagates them through a 3D volume. A4-MR uses it as the representative baseline for 3D organ localization, because the authors know of no public model that predicts organ bounding boxes directly from full 3D MRI volumes.

  • We therefore compare against MedSAM2 [23], a promptable medical foundation model that propagates 2D box prompts through the volume.
