Citing: ReXGroundingCT: A 3D Chest CT Dataset for Segmentation of Findings from Free-Text Reports

Open-Ended CT Volume Segmentation with Weak Supervision from Language

Sanjay Subramanian, Junwei Yu, Zirui Wang, Rohil Malpani et al. (8 authors) · 2026

What they say: Based on the abstract, ReXGroundingCT is used as an evaluation benchmark to demonstrate the results of their text-conditioned CT segmentation approach, with the dataset serving to validate that combining voxel-level supervision with weak slice-level supervision from radiology reports improves segmentation performance.
