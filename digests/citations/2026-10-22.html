Citing: ReXGroundingCT: A 3D Chest CT Dataset for Segmentation of Findings from Free-Text Reports

XGLASS: X-Ray, Gaze and Language Assisted Slice Sequencing for CT Volumes

M. Glöckler, T. Ropinski · 2026

What they say: ReXGroundingCT is, to the authors' knowledge, the only large-scale CT dataset that satisfies both of their requirements: a volumetric chest CT dataset whose disease findings match those of the REFLACX chest X-ray eye-tracking dataset, so that 2D gaze importance maps can be paired with 3D volumes. XGLASS therefore uses it as the CT source for training and evaluating its slice-prioritization method.
