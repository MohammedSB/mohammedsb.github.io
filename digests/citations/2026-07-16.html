Citing: ReXGroundingCT: A 3D Chest CT Dataset for Segmentation of Findings from Free-Text Reports

Decouple and Reason: Anatomically Guided Two-Stage Voxel-Level Grounding of Free-Text Findings in 3D Chest CT

Kwang-Hyun Uhm, Inhwa Son, Sung-Jea Ko · 2026

What they say: ReXGroundingCT is described as the recently released, first publicly available benchmark that establishes explicit linkages between free-text radiological findings and pixel-level (voxel-level) 3D segmentations in chest CT, and it provides the official leaderboard on which the citing method reports state-of-the-art overall grounding performance. It is invoked to motivate the task—free-text reports lack direct spatial mapping to visual anatomy—and to note that on this benchmark existing end-to-end approaches show suboptimal performance, often failing to detect subtle lesions or align them correctly with the text.
