LoRCA: LoRA Cycle Adaptation for Histology to HiP-CT Translation with DINOv3

Yang Zhou, Edoardo Occhipinti, Banboye Kidzeru Elvis, Jishizhan Chen et al. (14 authors) · 2026

What they say: DINOv2 and similar general-purpose vision foundation models are cited as producing feature maps that capture semantic structure, making them useful for tasks requiring morphology to be preserved across domains — the motivating premise for using DINOv3 features in histology-to-HiP-CT translation, though running a separate full foundation model per modality is dismissed as computationally expensive.

  • Although these foundation models generate feature maps of semantic structure and are beneficial to the tasks that require preservation of morphology across domains [1], deploying a full foundation model independently for each modality is computationally expensive.
